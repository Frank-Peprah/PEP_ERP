# Ghana tax rules governance (Release 4 — not yet implemented)

**No tax rates, thresholds or rules are implemented or seeded.** Accounts carry a `tax_mapping_code` placeholder.

Every rule added in R4 must record: official source (Act / L.I. / GRA practice note), effective-from/to,
version, reviewer (qualified Ghanaian tax practitioner), and last-verified date. Rules are effective-dated and
selected by transaction/period date. Exported packs are labelled DRAFT until reviewed; "filed" status requires
external submission evidence. No automation of GRA portal logins or CAPTCHA.
